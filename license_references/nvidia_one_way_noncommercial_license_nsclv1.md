# NVIDIA License (One-Way Noncommercial, "NSCLv1"-style)

Full text saved as: `nvidia_one_way_noncommercial_license_nsclv1.pdf` (fetched from
https://developer.download.nvidia.com/licenses/NVIDIA-OneWay-Noncommercial-License-22Mar2022.pdf).

Also referred to elsewhere as the "NVIDIA Source Code License (NC)." Used by 3 models in the NGC
physical-AI list (e.g. Mask Grounding DINO v2 / "NVIDIA License (Non-Commercial)").

## Commercial Use — asymmetric, NVIDIA-only carve-out
> Section 3.3, "Use Limitation": "The Work and any derivative works thereof only may be used or intended
for use non-commercially. **Notwithstanding the foregoing, NVIDIA Corporation and its affiliates may use the
Work and any derivative works commercially.** As used herein, 'non-commercially' means for research or
evaluation purposes only."

This is the genuine NVIDIA-specific lock: NVIDIA itself is explicitly exempted from the restriction that
binds every other company, including AMD. It is not a generic non-commercial license — it is a license
that is commercial for NVIDIA and non-commercial for everyone else.

## Redistribution
Permitted under the same license (Section 3.1), with a full copy of the license included and all
notices retained. Derivative works may carry additional terms (Section 3.2) but the non-commercial-use
limitation and redistribution requirements still apply to the original Work.

## Hardware Restriction
None stated — the restriction is on commercial use, not on which hardware it runs on.

## Patent clause
Section 3.4: bringing a patent claim against any Licensor terminates your rights under the license
immediately.
