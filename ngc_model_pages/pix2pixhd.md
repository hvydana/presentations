# pix2pixHD
Source: https://catalog.ngc.nvidia.com/orgs/nvidia/models/pix2pixhd

**Description/Overview:**
"Pytorch implementation of our method for high-resolution (e.g. 2048x1024) photorealistic image-to-image translation." The model implements the research presented in "High-Resolution Image Synthesis and Semantic Manipulation with Conditional GANs" (CVPR 2018).

**Use Cases:**
The model can be applied to converting semantic label maps into photorealistic imagery or generating portraits derived from facial label maps.

**Model Architecture:**
A deep learning-based conditional GAN approach designed for high-resolution image synthesis.
