The surge in artificial intelligence is generating significant gains for some companies, though not every associated stock promises equal investor returns. According to Yahoo Finance, Nvidia and Advanced Micro Devices are prominent forces in this technological shift, both experiencing intense demand for their semiconductor products.
Nvidia has emerged as a primary beneficiary of the ongoing build-out of AI infrastructure. Its graphics processing units serve as the main hardware for training the large language models that underpin AI systems. This role has driven substantial revenue growth for the company. From a market valuation perspective, Nvidia has grown to become the world's largest corporation. Its commanding position in AI model training is reinforced by its proprietary software platform, where a majority of foundational AI code is developed and optimized. The company reportedly holds an overwhelming share of the GPU market. It is also expanding its capabilities, including through recent licensing and hiring activities related to specialized inference technology.
Advanced Micro Devices, while historically holding a secondary role in the data center GPU sector, is aligning itself with key emerging AI trends such as inference and agentic AI. Inference is anticipated to grow into a larger market than model training over time. AMD has secured major GPU supply agreements with two leading purchasers of AI infrastructure, OpenAI and Meta Platforms. These agreements are expected to generate hundreds of millions in additional revenue for AMD. A condition of these deals involves the integration of AMD's competing software platform into the customers' ecosystems. The arrangements also provide an incentive for the customers to support AMD's ongoing development.
