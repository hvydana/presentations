Industrial cyber security: consultancy, testing and certification in accordance with IEC 62443

We support manufacturers, integrators and operators of plant and systems with accredited assessments of industrial cyber security in automation and control systems. We assess functional safety and cyber security as part of a single process.

Overview of key services:

  • Consultancy, risk analyses and gap analyses in accordance with IEC 62443
  • Accredited testing and certification of components, systems and processes
  • Combined assessment in accordance with IEC 62443, IEC 61508 and ISO 13849
  • Preparation for NIS-2, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Machinery Directive
  • CICSP and CICSE personal certifications, as well as training courses

Industrial cyber security for manufacturers, integrators and operators of plant and systems

Manufacturer

You develop and market industrial products, components or machinery.
Your requirements: Demonstration of a secure development process and secure products in accordance with IEC 62443-4-1 and 4-2. Preparation for the Cyber Resilience Act and the Machinery Regulation.
Our services: Assessment of your development process, testing of your products, certification in conjunction with functional safety.

System integrators and service providers

You integrate, maintain or operate industrial automation solutions for your customers.
Your requirements: Demonstration of a security programme in accordance with IEC 62443-2-4 and assessment of your system solutions in accordance with IEC 62443-3-3. A demonstrable advantage in tenders.
Our services: Audit of your integration programme, system assessment, certification.
 

Operators of plant and systems

You operate production facilities, critical infrastructure or networked industrial environments.
Your requirements: NIS 2-compliant risk management, implementation of a Cyber Security Management System in accordance with IEC 62443-2-1, risk analysis in accordance with IEC 62443-3-2.
Our services: Gap analysis, CSMS implementation, auditable documentation, surveillance.

Here’s what SGS-TÜV Saar can offer you

Accredited cyber security inspection body

Our assessments carry regulatory weight. As an accredited body, we test components, systems and management processes in accordance with IEC 62443, issuing certificates that are recognised in tender processes and by regulatory authorities.

Functional safety and cyber security from a single source

We assess IEC 62443 in conjunction with IEC 61508 and ISO 13849 as part of a single process. The same assessors consider safety and security together. This reduces audit time and closes any gaps at the interface between the two disciplines.

Interdisciplinary teams of safety and security experts

Our assessors combine expertise in functional safety, automation technology and information security. They have an equal understanding of control systems, development processes and management systems.

Part of the international SGS network

With 100,000 employees and over 2,500 offices worldwide, SGS has the reach required for international projects. We draw on specialists in the relevant markets to carry out audits and assessments.

Consultancy for industrial cyber security

We support you in planning and implementing your cyber security requirements. Our consultancy services accompany both the development and operational phases and are based on IEC 62443 and related standards.

At the end of a consultancy engagement, you will have a clear understanding of your current status, a prioritised list of measures and an actionable plan that also prepares you for future certification. We tailor our approach to your specific role as a manufacturer, integrator or plant operator.

Our consultancy services:

  • Risk analyses in accordance with IEC 62443-3-2 using the zone and conduit concept
  • Gap analyses against IEC 62443 for manufacturers, integrators and operators
  • Establishment of a Cyber Security Management System in accordance with IEC 62443-2-1
  • Consultancy throughout the development process in accordance with IEC 62443-4-1
  • Preparation for NIS-2, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Machinery Regulation

Assessment in accordance with IEC 62443 – Cyber security for your industrial plants

As an accredited inspection body, we carry out security audits and conformity assessments of your concepts, products, systems and processes. Unlike a purely consultative assessment, an accredited assessment meets the formal requirements specified in tenders, by regulatory authorities and within the framework of conformity assessments.

We assess your functional safety and cyber security requirements together. The same assessors evaluate both safety and security in a single process. This avoids duplicate audits and closes any gaps at the interface between the two disciplines.

Our audits:

  • IEC 62443-2-1 for management systems
  • IEC 62443-2-4 for requirements for integrators and service providers
  • IEC 62443-3-2 for risk analysis, zones and conduits
  • IEC 62443-3-3 for system requirements
  • IEC 62443-4-1 for development processes
  • IEC 62443-4-2 for product requirements
  • IEC 62443-4-1 and IEC 62443-4-2 in combination with IEC 61508 and ISO 13849 for safety components

Cybersecurity certifications

Following a successfully completed audit, we issue certificates that can help you build and strengthen your customers’ confidence in your products, systems or processes. As an accredited body, our certificates carry regulatory and market weight and are recognised in tenders, by regulatory authorities and by business partners.

A certification comprises the formal assessment, the issue of the certificate and the subsequent surveillance audits. The period of validity depends on the relevant certification scheme. We support you throughout the entire lifecycle, even after initial certification.

What we certify:

  • Components and products in accordance with IEC 62443-4-2
  • Development processes in accordance with IEC 62443-4-1
  • Systems in accordance with IEC 62443-3-3
  • Integrator programmes in accordance with IEC 62443-2-4
  • Cyber security management systems in accordance with IEC 62443-2-1

Cyber security for humanoid robots

Humanoid robots introduce new vulnerabilities into the connected industrial sector. As a manufacturer or integrator, you must not only demonstrate the functional safety of your system, but also ensure that interfaces, communication protocols and control systems are protected against attacks.

We support you in classifying and assessing your product’s cyber security requirements, from the initial consultation right through to certification in accordance with IEC 62443. In doing so, we also keep the requirements of the European Cyber Resilience Act in mind.

Excerpt from our standards:

  • IEC 62443-2-1, -2-4 for management systems
  • IEC 62443-3-2, -3-3 for systems
  • IEC 62443-4-1, -4-2 in combination with IEC 61508 for safety components
  • CRA basic requirements

Which obligations apply to you

NIS2 – Network and Information Security Directive 2 (EU Directive 2022/2555)

The NIS2 Implementation Act has been in force in Germany since December 2025. Affected organisations must register with the BSI, implement appropriate risk management and report security incidents within 24 hours. Senior management is personally liable.

We provide support with gap analyses, the establishment of a Cyber Security Management System in accordance with IEC 62443-2-1, and auditable documentation.

Cyber Resilience Act (EU) 2024/2847

Manufacturers of products containing digital elements must demonstrate cyber security throughout the entire life cycle. This includes conformity assessment, vulnerability management and update obligations.

We assess development processes and products in accordance with IEC 62443-4-1 and IEC 62443-4-2 in preparation for CRA compliance.

Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230

From 20 January 2027, cybersecurity will be a mandatory component of the conformity assessment for machinery. Manufacturers must demonstrate, through design and documentation, protective measures against foreseeable cyber-attacks.

We assess cybersecurity in conjunction with functional safety as part of a single procedure, thereby covering both requirements.

The project process in five steps

1. Initial consultation and scoping

We will clarify your role (manufacturer, integrator or operator), the scope, the relevant version of the standard and the target security level. You will receive a detailed quotation setting out the costs and the process.

2. Gap analysis

We assess your current status against the requirements of the relevant parts of IEC 62443. The result is a documented comparison of the target and current status, with prioritised recommendations for action.

3. Consultancy and support with implementation

We support your team in implementing the measures, setting up your cyber security management system or adapting your processes as development progresses. The duration and scope of our support will be tailored to your needs.

4. Examination and assessment

As an accredited inspection body, we review your documentation, carry out on-site audits and assess your technical and organisational measures. You will receive an inspection report.

5. Certification and monitoring

If the assessment is successful, we will issue the certificate. We will then support you with surveillance audits throughout the certificate’s validity period.
