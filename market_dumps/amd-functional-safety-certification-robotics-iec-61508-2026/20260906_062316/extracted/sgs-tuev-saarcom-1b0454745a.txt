We support manufacturers, integrators and operators of plant and systems with accredited assessments of industrial cyber security in automation and control systems. We assess functional safety and cyber security as part of a single process.
Overview of key services:
- Consultancy, risk analyses and gap analyses in accordance with IEC 62443
- Accredited testing and certification of components, systems and processes
- Combined assessment in accordance with IEC 62443, IEC 61508 and ISO 13849
- Preparation for NIS-2, the Cyber Resilience Act and the Machinery Directive
- CICSP and CICSE personal certifications, as well as training courses

        Humanoid robots introduce new vulnerabilities into the connected industrial sector. As a manufacturer or integrator, you must not only demonstrate the functional safety of your system, but also ensure that interfaces, communication protocols and control systems are protected against attacks.
We support you in classifying and assessing your product’s cyber security requirements, from the initial consultation right through to certification in accordance with IEC 62443. In doing so, we also keep the requirements of the European Cyber Resilience Act in mind.
Excerpt from our standards:
- IEC 62443-2-1, -2-4 for management systems
- IEC 62443-3-2, -3-3 for systems
- IEC 62443-4-1, -4-2 in combination with IEC 61508 for safety components
- CRA basic requirements
The NIS2 Implementation Act has been in force in Germany since December 2025. Affected organisations must register with the BSI, implement appropriate risk management and report security incidents within 24 hours. Senior management is personally liable.
We provide support with gap analyses, the establishment of a Cyber Security Management System in accordance with IEC 62443-2-1, and auditable documentation.
Manufacturers of products containing digital elements must demonstrate cyber security throughout the entire life cycle. This includes conformity assessment, vulnerability management and update obligations.
We assess development processes and products in accordance with IEC 62443-4-1 and IEC 62443-4-2 in preparation for CRA compliance.
From 20 January 2027, cybersecurity will be a mandatory component of the conformity assessment for machinery. Manufacturers must demonstrate, through design and documentation, protective measures against foreseeable cyber-attacks.
We assess cybersecurity in conjunction with functional safety as part of a single procedure, thereby covering both requirements.

As an accredited inspection body, we review your documentation, carry out on-site audits and assess your technical and organisational measures. You will receive an inspection report.
