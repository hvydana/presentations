From August 19, 2026 to August 23, 2026
World Robot Conference (WRC) – Global Robotics & AI Summit
Overview:
The World Robot Conference is a major international robotics and artificial intelligence event that showcases the latest breakthroughs in AI-enabled robotics, industrial automation, humanoid systems, smart manufacturing, and robotic applications. It integrates high-level forums, exhibitions, contests, and side events designed to foster innovation, industrial cooperation and global technological exchange.
Topics Covered:
- Cutting-edge robotics and AI technologies
- Industrial automation and smart manufacturing
- Human-robot interaction and embodied intelligence
- Standards and regulatory frameworks
- Robotic competitions and innovation showcases
- Advanced Robotics & Automation Symposium (WRC SARA)
Location & Time: Beijing, China (Beiren Etrong International Exhibition & Convention Center)
2026 Edition: August 19–23, 2026
Target Participants:
- Robotics industry leaders and executives
- AI and automation researchers
- Engineers and product developers
- Government representatives & policy makers
- Academics, students, and technical innovators
- Investors and business development professionals
Core Components:
- Main Forum & Keynotes: Global expert presentations and trend analysis
- World Robot Expo: Exhibitions of robotics and AI innovations
- World Robot Contest: Competitive robotics events with global participation
- Symposium on Advanced Robotics & Automation (WRC SARA): Technical papers and research exchange
Registration:
Main Forum and Exhibition access (single-day or full pass) – tickets available via official registration portal
Symposium (WRC SARA) academic registration available with IEEE member/student pricing options
Official links for registration are accessible through the World Robot Conference official site → Main Forum Registration & WRC SARA Registration pages. https://www.worldrobotconference.com/en/forum/registration/
