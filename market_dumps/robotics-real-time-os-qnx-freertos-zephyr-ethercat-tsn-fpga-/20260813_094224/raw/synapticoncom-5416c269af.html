POSITRON SAFETY AI:
FUNCTIONAL SAFETY FOR AI-BASED ROBOTICS AND MACHINES 

WHITE PAPER

What is POSITRON SAFETY AI? We present Synapticon’s AI-based safety architecture for humanoid robots—the first certifiable solution that simultaneously addresses mechanical instability and non-deterministic AI behavior. The new white paper “Out of the Cage: Advanced Functional Safety for Humanoids” by Dr.-Ing. Florian Weißhardt, Dr.-Ing. Tim Fröhlich, and Dieter Volpert describes the architecture in detail.
Download
Synapticon’s POSITRON SAFETY AI platform is revolutionizing industrial safety by advancing the functional safety of AI-controlled robots and machines. It combines state-of-the-art hardware and software solutions to ensure reliable and robust safety functions that meet the highest industrial standards.

Whether you’re working on advanced robotics, industrial automation, or AI-controlled machines, Synapticon’s POSITRON SAFETY AI ensures a fail-safe and reliable environment without compromising performance.

Comprehensive functional safety:

POSITRON SAFETY AI is designed in accordance with SIL3 PLe (ARM) standards and integrates motion safety, human detection, and behavioral safety into a single device to prevent both risks arising from intentional movements (such as the robot deliberately injuring people) and unintentional hazards (accidental injury caused by the robot).

POSITRON SAFETY AI STN1

Safety hardware target for behavioral safety AI, safe person detection and high-performance motion safety
‍Thearchitecture of the IGX Jetson Thor follows the NVIDIA Halos Principles and incorporates safety concepts derived from NVIDIA’s research into autonomous driving. The system is designed to meet the hardware requirements of ISO 26262 (Automotive Functional Safety) and IEC 61508 (Functional Safety) and supports the implementation of additional safety standards such as ISO 13849 and ISO/IEC TS 22440. This allows developers to leverage pre-certified safety components and significantly reduce development and certification efforts.
‍
The central safety element is a Functional Safety Island (FSI) featuring a lockstep-based multicore MCU with its own clock, power, and memory domains. This FSI is completely isolated from the application computing platform and includes watchdog timers, ECC-protected memory areas, CRC mechanisms, and direct actuator interfaces. This allows hard real-time fail-safe and fail-operational functions to be executed independently of the main system.
‍
POSITRON SAFETY AI uses this architecture to implement safety-critical AI applications in a deterministic, traceable, and standards-compliant manner. This makes the use of complex AI models in embodied AI systems—from collaborative robots to autonomous machines—functionally safe and certifiable.
  • Nvidia IGX Jetson Thor SOM-based
  • All-in-one AI high-performance PC
  • MPC + Motion Control CPU and comprehensive safety controller
  • Flexible safety-certified IOs, safety-certified IMU
