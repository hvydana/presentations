Synapticon’s POSITRON SAFETY AI platform is revolutionizing industrial safety by advancing the functional safety of AI-controlled robots and machines. Thearchitecture of the IGX Jetson Thor follows the NVIDIA Halos Principles and incorporates safety concepts derived from NVIDIA’s research into autonomous driving. The system is designed to meet the hardware requirements of ISO 26262 (Automotive Functional Safety) and IEC 61508 (Functional Safety) and supports the implementation of additional safety standards such as ISO 13849 and ISO/IEC TS 22440. This allows developers to leverage pre-certified safety components and significantly reduce development and certification efforts.
The central safety element is a Functional Safety Island (FSI) featuring a lockstep-based multicore MCU with its own clock, power, and memory domains. This FSI is completely isolated from the application computing platform and includes watchdog timers, ECC-protected memory areas, CRC mechanisms, and direct actuator interfaces. This allows hard real-time fail-safe and fail-operational functions to be executed independently of the main system.
POSITRON SAFETY AI uses this architecture to implement safety-critical AI applications in a deterministic, traceable, and standards-compliant manner. This makes the use of complex AI models in embodied AI systems—from collaborative robots to autonomous machines—functionally safe and certifiable.
