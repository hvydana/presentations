Why LTX

**From simulation** to action

One open foundation for every stage of the physical AI journey, from simulation to world-action models.

Generate synthetic data

Create diverse environments, viewpoints, lighting conditions, and object interactions to expand robotics training datasets.

Adapt to your environment

Fine-tune LTX on proprietary environments, sensor data, and robotics tasks using IC-LoRA.

Deploy with efficiency

Run inference in near real time on a single GPU: on-prem, in your cloud, or at the edge where robots actually operate.

Build toward world-action models

Start with synthetic data and simulation today. The same open foundation is built to extend toward real-time policy generation, without switching stacks when you get there.
