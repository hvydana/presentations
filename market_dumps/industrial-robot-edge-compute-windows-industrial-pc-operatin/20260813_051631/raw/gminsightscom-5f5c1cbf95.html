The market growth is driven by rising demand for automation solutions, advanced manufacturing and industrial internet of things (IIoT) solutions, creating a significant opportunity for investment in high performance and reliable industrial PCs to support operational efficiency and digital transformation on a global scale through new technologies such as smart factory initiatives and cloud-based data processing.
 

The use of industrial PCs in the manufacturing, energy and logistics industries, the rising demand for reliable control solutions, real-time data processing and seamless integration with automated equipment will create opportunities for industrial PC sales and other high-performance computing solutions. For instance, in September 2025, Darveen partnered with Logicbus to expand industrial computing solutions in the U.S. and Mexico.
 

In IIoT ecosystems, industrial PCs serve as the collection point and processing center for industrial internet-connected devices and sensors. The rise in industrial internet of things (IIoT) use cases such as predictive maintenance, process optimization, and asset management, has led to increased demand for high-performance, rugged, and scalable industrial computing solutions within many industries. For instance, in December 2025, Asus partnered with CTHINGS.CO’s software to develop AI-driven IoT deployments and data insight.
 

As manufacturers continue to roll out various smart factory initiatives, industrial PCs expected to play a vital role by allowing manufacturers to interconnect their production systems, robotics systems, and monitoring systems (often referred to as interconnected smart factory or Industry 4.0), thus allowing manufacturers to perform real-time analytics on their production systems, facilitate greater operational efficiencies and support advanced technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) on a larger scale in the automotive, electronics and heavy industries markets. For instance, in January 2026, NEURA Robotics partnered with Bosch to support the industrial deployment of humanoid robotics and Physical AI from Germany.
 

Between 2022 and 2024, the industrial PC market experienced significant growth, rising from USD 5 billion in 2022 to USD 5.2 billion in 2024. A major trend during this period was that industrial PCs are becoming more used in harshly high-temperature environments because they’ve been built to withstand these conditions. Because of their ability to tolerate extreme temperature change, vibration, or high heat, and their dust-resistant properties, these computers have become a standard in mission-critical applications and continue to have strong sales growth within the market. For instance, in April 2022, Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL) partnered with Microsoft to accelerate the firm’s digital transformation and shaping the future of innovation in the oil and gas industry.
 

As artificial intelligence (AI) and edge computing become integrated with industrial computer hardware, companies will be able to analyse large amounts of real-time data, make informed decisions based on that analysis (using predictive models), and implement automation technology resulting in increased efficiency. These trends benefit the manufacturing sector by providing lower latency for operations, increased processing capability for industrial data (i.e. increasing speed), and support for the adoption of more advanced industrial computer solutions globally. For instance, in January 2026, Advantech partnered with Intel Core Ultra series 3 processor to develop Edge AI computers.
 

Manufacturing and logistics businesses are now using hybrid IT solutions and cloud processing to use the new capabilities provided by industrial computer technology to monitor, store, and manage all types of physical objects from anywhere at any time with one common user interface through the Web or smartphone applications. As businesses adopt these types of IT solutions, there’s a huge increase in the need for industrial PCs. For instance, in November 2025, Deutsche Telekom partnered with NVIDIA to launch Industrial AI Cloud.

Industrial PC Market Analysis


The Industrial PC market was valued at USD 5 billion and USD 5.1 billion in 2022 and 2023, respectively. The market size reached USD 5.3 billion in 2025, growing from USD 5.2 billion in 2024.
 

Based on type, the market is segmented into Panel IPC, Rack Mount IPC, Embedded IPC, DIN Rail IPC, and Others. The Panel IPC segment is estimated to register a significant growth rate of over 41.6% of the market in 2025.
 

  • The Panel IPC segment holds the largest share in the industrial PC market, driven by increasing adoption of all in one touch screen solutions that include both display and computer capabilities is increasing exponentially. A benefit of this solution is that they reduce complexity of installation, decrease footprint, improve human to machine interaction and allow for monitoring and controlling processes in real-time. Touch screen solutions are helping improve productivity in many industries including manufacturing, healthcare and automation.
     
  • Manufacturers should focus on introduction of more sophisticated panel IPCs that provide high resolution touch screen capabilities, increased computing performance and reliable connectivity, industries will benefit from user-friendly interfaces and durable panel IPCs that can be integrated into IoT and automation applications. This will result in improved productivity, and enhanced market position amongst the many sectors currently utilizing Panel IPCs including manufacturing, healthcare and industrial automation.
     
  • The DIN Rail IPC segment in the industrial PC market is anticipated to witness significant growth, projected to expand at a CAGR of 6.7% during the forecast period. this growth is driven by increasing demand for small, modular, and durable computing solutions that can easily be mounted on din rails has led to the development of compact modular din rail industrial Pcs. designed to handle the harshest environments, these IPCs are able to support real-time data acquisition, enable seamless integration with automation and control systems, provide a low-cost option for the manufacturing, energy, transport and smart infrastructure markets.
     
  • manufacturers should focus on designing compact modular din rail IPCs with reliable performance in harsh industrial environments it is important to focus on real-time data acquisition, seamless integration and cost-effective solutions. By providing these capabilities in compact modular DIN Rail IPCs manufacturers will be able to capitalize on the growth of the manufacturing, energy, transportation and smart infrastructure.

This report draws on a structured research process built around direct industry conversations, proprietary modelling, and rigorous cross-validation and not just desk research.

Our 6-step research process

  1. 1. Research design & analyst oversight

    At GMI, our research methodology is built on a foundation of human expertise, rigorous validation, and complete transparency. Every insight, trend analysis, and forecast in our reports is developed by experienced analysts who understand the nuances of your market.

    Our approach integrates extensive primary research through direct engagement with industry participants and experts, complemented by comprehensive secondary research from verified global sources. We apply quantified impact analysis to deliver dependable forecasts, while maintaining complete traceability from original data sources to final insights.

  2. 2. Primary research

    Primary research forms the backbone of our methodology, contributing nearly 80% to overall insights. It involves direct engagement with industry participants to ensure accuracy and depth in analysis. Our structured interview program covers regional and global markets, with inputs from C-suite executives, directors, and subject matter experts. These interactions provide strategic, operational, and technical perspectives, enabling well-rounded insights and reliable market forecasts.

  3. 3. Data mining & market analysis

    Data mining is a key part of our research process, contributing nearly 20% to the overall methodology. It involves analysing market structure, identifying industry trends, and assessing macroeconomic factors through revenue share analysis of major players. Relevant data is collected from both paid and unpaid sources to build a reliable database. This information is then integrated to support primary research and market sizing, with validation from key stakeholders such as distributors, manufacturers, and associations.

  4. 4. Market sizing

    Our market sizing is built on a bottom-up approach, starting with company revenue data gathered directly through primary interviews, alongside production volume figures from manufacturers and installation or deployment statistics. These inputs are then pieced together across regional markets to arrive at a global estimate that stays grounded in actual industry activity.

  5. 5. Forecast model & key assumptions

    Every forecast includes explicit documentation of:

    • ✓ Key growth drivers and their assumed impact

    • ✓ Restraining factors and mitigation scenarios

    • ✓ Regulatory assumptions and policy change risk

    • ✓ Technology adoption curve parameter

    • ✓ Macroeconomic assumptions (GDP growth, inflation, currency)

    • ✓ Competitive dynamics and market entry/exit expectations

  6. 6. Validation & quality assurance

    The final stages involve human validation, where domain experts manually review filtered data to identify nuances and contextual errors that automated systems might miss. This expert review adds a critical layer of quality assurance, ensuring data aligns with research objectives and domain-specific standards.
