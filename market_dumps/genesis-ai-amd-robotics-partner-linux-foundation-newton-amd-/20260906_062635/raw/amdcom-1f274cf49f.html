    Fast, Efficient Processing, From Real-Time Control to AI Reasoning

    As robotics evolve from programmed automation to autonomous physical AI systems, robots require a new class of compute that combines AI reasoning, real-time control, sensor processing, deterministic execution, and safety.

    AMD delivers the industry's broadest embedded portfolio, from high-performance x86 processors that power robot reasoning and planning, to adaptive computing that enables sensor fusion, real-time networking, motor control, and functional safety. Combined with open software and developer platforms, AMD helps accelerate robotics development and deployment across many applications, including industrial automation, logistics, and healthcare.

    Leadership AI Compute

    High-performance CPU, GPU and AI acceleration for perception, reasoning, planning, and multi-agent AI.

    Secure, Deterministic, Real-time Control

    Deliver precise motion, functional safety, and low-latency control for robots operating in dynamic environments.

    Open & Scalable

    Scale across robot architectures with a broad portfolio of AMD x86 embedded processors, adaptive SoCs, FPGAs, SOMs, and open software.

    Applications

    Only AMD Delivers the Complete Autonomous Robotics Solution from Body to Brain

    Brain

    Perception and Reasoning 

    AMD embedded APUs can enable perception pipelines and generative AI while delivering the reliability and longevity required for robots and autonomous industrial vehicles. Combine real-time CPU control, fast GPU inference, and agentic performance, and replace fragmented architectures with a single Kria™ AI SOM.

    Spine

    Industrial Automation

    AMD adaptive SoCs and FPGAs allow the distribution of robotic workloads into the best architecture for your needs and to manage robot safety and security.

    Joints

    Motor Control & Predictive Maintenance

    AMD adaptive SoCs and FPGAs are used for deterministic real-time control of robot arms and joints.

    Sensing

    Vision AI, LiDAR, & Sensors

    AMD embedded APU, adaptive SoC, and FPGA products accelerate machine vision, LiDAR, and real-time sensor processing to help improve perception, extend detection range, and enable safe autonomous operation.

    Partner Spotlight: Generative Bionics

    Building Humanoids That Can React to Touch

    Generative Bionics is leveraging an open and scalable robotics platform from AMD to build humanoid robots that can sense and react to their environment in real-time. The company's GENE-01 robot takes human-machine intelligence to the next level with sensing, decision-making, and action that provides a glimpse into the future of autonomous robotics. AMD has invested in the company to help it scale toward industrial production, expanding real-world deployments across many industries.
