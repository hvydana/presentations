Formula 1 2026: 22 Drivers, Cadillac F1, and a Thrilling Title Fight
The 2026 Formula 1 season promises to be one of the most exciting yet. With the entry of Cadillac F1, a total of 22 drivers will take to the grid, bringing fresh talent, returning veterans, and intense competition for the world championship.
All eyes are on McLaren, which dominated the 2025 season, with Lando Norris as the reigning world champion. However, Max Verstappen and Red Bull Racing are determined to challenge for the title with their new Red Bull Powertrains engine, while Isack Hadjar joins as Verstappen’s key teammate.
Ferrari and Mercedes are also aiming for success, relying on top drivers like Charles Leclerc, Lewis Hamilton, and George Russell. Meanwhile, Arvid Lindblad makes his Formula 1 debut with Racing Bulls, and seasoned drivers Sergio Pérez and Valtteri Bottas return to lead the new Cadillac F1 team.
With a mix of rising stars, experienced drivers, and a new factory team, Formula 1 2026 is set to deliver speed, strategy, and thrilling battles from start to finish.
