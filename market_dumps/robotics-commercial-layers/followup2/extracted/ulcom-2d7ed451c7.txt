Automotive systems are becoming increasingly complex and connected, making functional safety compliance essential for protecting lives and meeting regulatory requirements. Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), autonomous functions, electrification and software-defined vehicle architectures place growing demands on safety-related computing, sensing and control technologies. As these functions increasingly rely on advanced semiconductor devices, embedded software and highly integrated systems, supporting functional safety at both the component and system levels has become a critical success factor across the automotive value chain.
ISO 26262 is a globally recognized framework for addressing functional safety in road vehicles. It covers safety‑critical electrical and electronic systems, including both hardware and software, and establishes a structured approach to identifying and reducing risks associated with system failures throughout the vehicle life cycle.
