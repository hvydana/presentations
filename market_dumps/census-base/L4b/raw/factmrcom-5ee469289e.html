What is accelerating humanoid robot fleet management services adoption, and what is holding it back?

Commercial humanoid pilots are the main driver. GXO and Agility Robotics signed a multi-year Robots-as-a-Service agreement in 2024 to deploy Digit humanoid robots in logistics operations, with Agility Arc supporting the deployment lifecycle and troubleshooting [4]. Each deployment creates demand for commissioning and maintenance services.

The main restraint is operational uncertainty. Humanoids still face variable task performance and facility-specific workflow challenges. Operators may delay larger deployments if robots need too much human intervention. Service providers must prove that remote support and maintenance can reduce downtime enough to justify recurring fees.
