Client success story

Situation

Following an acquisition, a Canadian client was under pressure to reduce costs quickly. As part of a short-term savings initiative, 200 sales vehicles were removed from the fleet and drivers were transitioned to a reimbursement model. While intended to lower costs, the shift created uncertainty around long-term fleet efficiency and total cost of ownership.

Solution

Element partnered with the client to conduct a detailed lease-versus-reimbursement analysis using actual operating data. The analysis demonstrated that leasing was $0.21 per mile more cost-effective than reimbursement. Element also identified drivers who were not eligible for reimbursement programs, providing additional clarity to support decision-making and reinforce the financial case for leasing.

Impact

The analysis clearly validated leasing as the more cost-effective approach, delivering approximately $570K in annual total cost of ownership savings, equating to $1.7M over three years. The data-driven recommendation helped the client regain confidence in their fleet strategy while strengthening the long-term partnership.
