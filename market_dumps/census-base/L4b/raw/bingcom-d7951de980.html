Catalyst AI looks at the unique makeup of your fleet, including vehicle type, size and how you operate – to understand your fleet’s “DNA.” Using advanced data analysis and insights from hundreds of thousands of vehicles, Catalyst AI finds other fleets that run in a similar way. This gives you accurate, apples-to-apples comparisons you can trust.

Because every fleet runs differently, comparing your performance to a truly similar group gives you a more accurate picture of how you’re doing. Catalyst AI helps you see where you’re leading the pack and where there’s room to improve – so you can make smarter decisions about your performance and overall efficiency.

All Penske customers get free access to the fleet level features powered by Catalyst AI through Comparative Insights, a feature in Fleet Insight. If you want to explore the premium version with deeper, unit level and hub level analysis, you can try it for free for 30 days – no credit card or billing information is required, and you can cancel at any time.

Customers can access Comparative Insights, powered by Catalyst AI, by doing the following:

  1. Log in to Fleet Insight web.
  2. Open the left-hand menu.
  3. Select Comparative Insights.
  4. Choose Performance Comparison.
  5. You’ll now see your Comparative Insights dashboard. To activate your free premium trial (deeper insights), click the yellow “Free Premium Trial” button on the right.
