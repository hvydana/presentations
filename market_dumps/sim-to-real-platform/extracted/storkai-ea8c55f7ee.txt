What is Gazebo?
Gazebo is a robot simulator tool developed by the Gazebo Project that enables robotics developers and researchers to design, test, and validate robotic systems in virtual environments. It provides robust physics, high-quality graphics, and extensive sensor modeling, integrating with ROS/ROS 2 for comprehensive simulation. This open-source platform facilitates rapid iteration on design concepts and control strategies within realistic environments, supporting various robotic systems including drones. It serves as a fundamental tool for robotics research, algorithm testing, and training AI models in virtual settings before real-world deployment.
