cuRobo: CUDA Accelerated Robot Library¶
[API] [GTC Talk] [Code] [Paper]
cuRobo is a CUDA-accelerated library for robot motion generation, built on PyTorch, CUDA, and Warp. It provides GPU-parallel algorithms for forward/inverse kinematics, collision checking, trajectory optimization, geometric planning, GPU-native perception, and whole-body motion generation, scaling from single-arm manipulators to high-DoF humanoids.
