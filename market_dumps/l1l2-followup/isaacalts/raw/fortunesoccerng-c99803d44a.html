RSK Pool Papers are weekly football pool forecasting sheets used by stakers to predict draws in the UK (English) and Aussie (Australia) football pools seasons. These papers provide compiled statistics, key sequence codes, and expert prediction to help you find the necessary score draws, no score draws, home win, away win and dead games on a coupon.

 

The three most prominent variants of these publications include:

  • RSK Capital International Paper: This sheet is heavily relied upon for tracking long-term sequence trends and specific “banker” draws.
  • RSK Bob Morton Paper: This version focuses heavily on compiled team forms, expert predictions, head to head records and dead-accurate pair selections.
  • RSK Soccer ‘X’ Research Paper: This journal comes with special x tips for the week and skippers statistics ratings for the week’s games.

 

Key Sections to Analyze in RSK Papers

– The Banker: The single most confident match tipped by the editors to result in a score draw.
– The Pairs: Groups of two matches where at least one is mathematically projected to draw based on historical sequence matrixes.
– The Summary Panel: A compiled voting block featuring predictions from various top pool tipsters.
– The Code Matrix: Alphabetical or numerical cyphers hidden within the match fixtures used by sequence forecast formula systems.
