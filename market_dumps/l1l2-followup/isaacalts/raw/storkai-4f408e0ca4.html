overview

What is CoppeliaSim?

CoppeliaSim is a robot simulation tool developed by Coppelia Robotics AG that enables robotics R&D, education, and automation professionals to create, compose, and simulate a wide array of robotic systems and complex environments. It provides an integrated development environment with extensive API support and multiple physics engines for virtual prototyping, testing, and verification of robotic designs and algorithms. Formerly known as V-REP, the platform is utilized across industry, education, and research for its versatility and scalability in simulating complex mechatronic systems.

features

Key Features of CoppeliaSim

CoppeliaSim offers a comprehensive suite of features designed for advanced robot simulation and development, supporting a wide range of applications from algorithm testing to digital twin creation.

  • Distributed control architecture for flexible system design.
  • Integration of 5 distinct physics engines: MuJoCo, Bullet Physics, ODE, Newton, and Vortex Dynamics.
  • Forward and Inverse Kinematics calculations for diverse mechanism types.
  • Volumetric proximity and vision sensor simulation with advanced image processing options.
  • Path and motion planning capabilities via the OMPL (Open Motion Planning Library).
  • Powerful APIs supporting 7 languages: Python, Lua, C/C++, Java, JavaScript, MATLAB, and Octave, plus Rust.
  • Native ROS interfaces for publishers, subscribers, and service calls.
  • Fast collision and distance calculation between various geometric shapes.
  • Extensive collection of plugins and add-ons for trajectory generation, custom UIs, and computer vision.
  • Browser-based viewer, multilevel undo/redo functionality, and a built-in movie recorder.

use cases

Who Should Use CoppeliaSim?

CoppeliaSim is designed for a broad spectrum of users involved in robotics and automation, providing tools for virtual prototyping, education, and system optimization.

  • Robotics R&D Engineers: For fast algorithm development, virtual prototyping, and verification of robotic designs before physical implementation.
  • Automation System Designers: To model and simulate entire factory layouts and robotic work cells, optimizing processes and verifying system behavior.
  • Educators and Students: As a widely used tool for teaching robotics concepts, practical applications, and conducting academic research.
  • Digital Twin Developers: To create virtual replicas of physical systems for remote monitoring, analysis, and safety double-checking.
  • Software Developers: Utilizing its extensive API support to integrate custom control logic and external applications with simulated environments.

how to use

How to Use CoppeliaSim

CoppeliaSim provides an integrated development environment for designing, simulating, and controlling robotic systems. Users typically begin by setting up their simulation environment and then developing control logic.

  • 1Download and install the CoppeliaSim software compatible with your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Ubuntu Linux).
  • 2Design or import 3D models of robots, sensors, and environmental elements into the simulation scene.
  • 3Configure robot kinematics, dynamics, and sensor properties using the integrated tools.
  • 4Develop control algorithms using embedded scripts (Lua, Python) or remote APIs (Python, C++, MATLAB, ROS) to interact with the simulated robot.
  • 5Run simulations to test robot behavior, validate algorithms, and analyze performance metrics.
  • 6Utilize features like the movie recorder or browser-based viewer for visualization and presentation of simulation results.

pricing

CoppeliaSim Pricing & Plans

CoppeliaSim operates on a dual-licensing model, offering both a free educational version and a commercial license with full functionality. Specific pricing for the commercial version requires direct inquiry.

  • CoppeliaSim Edu: Free. This license is fully functional but is exclusively for use by students, teachers, and professors of schools and universities for non-commercial purposes.
  • CoppeliaSim Pro / Full: Contact for pricing. This tier provides full simulation functionality, complete editing capabilities, commercial usage rights, and customer distribution packaging (available upon request).

Similar Tools

CoppeliaSim vs Competitors

CoppeliaSim is recognized for its versatility and extensive feature set within the robot simulation landscape, competing with several prominent platforms each with distinct strengths.

1
GazeboOn Stork

Gazebo is the most widely used open-source robot simulator, known for its realistic physics, sensor simulation, and seamless integration with ROS/ROS2, making it an industry standard for mobile robots, manipulators, and drones.

Unlike CoppeliaSim which is a paid product, Gazebo is free and open-source, offering a robust platform for robotics research and development with a large community and extensive documentation, though its graphics may be less realistic than some commercial alternatives.

2
WebotsOn Stork

Webots is a user-friendly, open-source 3D robot simulator that provides a complete development environment with a rich library of assets, making it ideal for education, academic research, and rapid prototyping.

Similar to CoppeliaSim in its versatility for prototyping and algorithm development, Webots is open-source and free, offering a more accessible entry point for beginners and supporting multiple programming languages including C, C++, Python, Java, MATLAB, and ROS.

3
NVIDIA Isaac Sim↗

Built on NVIDIA Omniverse, Isaac Sim is a high-fidelity, GPU-accelerated simulation platform specifically designed for AI-powered robotics, offering photorealistic rendering, synthetic data generation, and tight integration with AI frameworks for advanced robot learning and validation.

While CoppeliaSim offers multiple physics engines and broad API support, Isaac Sim excels in AI-driven robotics with superior graphics and synthetic data generation capabilities, and it is free to use for non-production purposes and open-source, though it requires substantial GPU resources.

4
MuJoCoOn Stork

MuJoCo is a high-performance physics engine renowned for its accurate robotic dynamics and fast simulation, making it a preferred choice for reinforcement learning research and complex manipulation tasks with contacts.

Unlike CoppeliaSim's integrated development environment and broad feature set, MuJoCo focuses primarily on highly accurate and fast physics simulation, often used as a backend for other robotics frameworks, and it is free and open-source.
