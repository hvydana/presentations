Genesis AI - About the company
Genesis AI is a seed company based in Palo Alto (United States), founded in 2025 by Theophile Gervet, Zhou Xian and Tsun-Hsuan Wang. It operates as a Global physical AI lab and full-stack robotics company. Genesis AI has raised $105M in funding from Khosla Ventures and Eclipse. The company has 37 active competitors, including 17 that are funded. Its top competitors include companies like Field AI, Scout AI and Physical Intelligence. 
Company Details
Global physical AI lab and full-stack robotics company. It build generalist robots to unlock unlimited physical labor, so humans can focus on creativity, curiosity. It built cross-platform GPU compilers such as VeriGPU, DeepCL, Coriander, and the original PyTorch, as well as core ML infra like HF safetensors, tokenizers, and text-generation-inference. We also built industry-leading rendering engines at Google, Epic and Unity. It fuses real-world robot interaction, high-fidelity physics simulation and rendering, and Internet-scale embodied data to train a universal robotics foundation model. It accelerates progress of the entire field through scalable simulation infrastructure and open-weight foundation models.
