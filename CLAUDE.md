# Voice AI Techno-Business Article Project

## Project Goal
Write a comprehensive techno-business article on Voice AI covering:
- Popular Voice AI applications and how they improve business
- Market size and growth expectations
- Full tech stack breakdown with cost analysis
- Where AMD can contribute (hardware gaps, cost-effective alternatives)
- Popular toolkits and what AMD is missing / can contribute
- India-specific business impact: hours saved, GDP contribution, day-to-day optimization

## Source URLs
All source URLs are in `techno_bussiness.md`. Use `scrape_urls.py` to download and extract text from them before writing.

## Article Structure
1. Introduction: Voice AI landscape 2026
2. Popular applications by vertical (healthcare, customer service, finance, etc.)
3. Business impact metrics
4. Tech stack layers (ASR → NLU → TTS → orchestration)
5. Cost breakdown per layer — where AMD hardware helps
6. AMD opportunity analysis
7. Popular toolkits (open-source + commercial) — AMD gaps and contributions
8. India market analysis: GDP impact, hours saved, adoption path

## Conventions
- Keep claims grounded in scraped source data
- Note when data is estimated vs cited
- Use the scraped text files in `scraped/` directory as primary sources
